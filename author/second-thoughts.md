# Second thoughts

A doubt about a level would ordinarily be written at that level's own foot. These stand at the root instead, because a large part of what this entry connects was written by sessions, and the author has neither read nor ratified all of it. A doubt of his own, filed deep in a file he may never open again, is a doubt that has disappeared.

So they stand here until they are answered or dropped, and nothing beneath has been changed to answer them.

*The author's, 2026-09-16, on his asking that they be placed far up; the session wrote them down.*

## 1. There is no visible way to fold what you have read

A reader can unfold a brief because they would rather preview it than enter it. They scroll through it, reach its end and meet the next piece, and there is nothing there to fold what they have just read. The keys can do it, [shift with the space bar](../poc/surface/lane.md#64-the-keys) folding the brief above, but no act on the page can, and on a phone there are no keys at all.

The author does not know how to solve it either.

*The author's, 2026-09-16; open.*

## 2. Some levels are entered and read, not unfolded

The author is having second thoughts about how much of the body may be unfolded.

Unfolding the rule from the rule is asking for a preview of it: what is the rule. But some of what stands inside the rule are parts of the rule, and a reader is meant to read them through. Entering it, you read it as one, and a brief of it standing folded while you are meant to read it reads strangely.

Where that line falls is what is in doubt. A holon comes with a file today, and that may not be it: sometimes a level deeper than a file should still be entered rather than previewed. The rule goes that deep in places, [the holarchy](../rule.md#321-the-holarchy) and [the link](../rule.md#322-the-link) beneath the brief, and those are details a reader may skip, which is the rule working as the rule says. But [what knowledge is made of](../rule.md#3-what-knowledge-is-made-of) and its three parts cannot be skipped by anyone who wants to understand it.

So what is owed is whether a level says somewhere that it is entered rather than unfolded, and on what that would be decided.

*The author's, 2026-09-16, thinking aloud while reading the rule on a phone; open, and nothing is built from it.*

# Ideas of 2026-09-18

## 1. What the pilot has shown, and who still cannot enter

The pilot may have proven some usefulness in the new experience of what it is to work with AI in a substrate, and in a substrate with the kind of ontological foundation that helps AI and human alike to collaborate.

The author knows the ontology and is experienced with working in a classical harness. Someone new may still not know the technique at all, how you open a session and the rest of it. That is definitely wanted, and it is not what comes first.

*The author's, 2026-09-18, dictated.*

## 2. The surface may be near the limit of its stack

The pilot has now grown in terms of the surface code, and it may hit a limit soon enough for how much can be done with it.

This was realised in trying the git history: the technological stack at hand just does not allow it to do anything. That is covered in [the latest edition of the ideas](../ideas/history.md).

*The author's, 2026-09-18, dictated.*

## 3. Ratification and comments need authentication, and stand further out

Being able to invite someone without a harness to ratify, and creating a good user experience and substrate integration for ratification, is wanted. So are possibly multiple types of measures that we may add ourselves.

Comments are kind of the same thing: you leave a comment with a certain amount of measures, or something like that.

But that means authentication, and it means quite a few things. They are steps somewhat more far away than where we are now.

*The author's, 2026-09-18, dictated; it stands beside [ratification is first class](ideas-2026-09-17.md#4-ratification-is-first-class) and [authentication individualises contributors](ideas-2026-09-17.md#5-authentication-individualises-contributors) from the day before.*

## 4. What the rule is for right now

Really, the rule right now is just to enable us to do an appropriate ontological tool-making process. Hjulverkstan is in this process, and so is our closed source project.

*The author's, 2026-09-18, dictated.*

## 5. Unfolding everything may break the linear whole

The way in which you navigate, that everything is unfoldable, might be problematic.

Something like the rule with its markdown file is made to be read in one stretch. At a certain point it may be automatically folded, but for the first two levels that is not really helpful. You are not really getting what you need to get.

The question might be: is it really following the rule? If you read the highest level, are you lost at something, or aren't you? And if you then choose to go back to the beginning and read one layer deeper, will you still have as strong an experience as you would if you had read it at two levels deep already?

So what is known is that some content is, as a whole, designed to be read linearly. And we may acknowledge that some is not. In the entry file we are now putting various holons, and they are not necessarily made to be read like that. There could be a difference if the H1 brief were actually in the file, and then maybe we would customise and build something ourselves. But does that really matter, for we would still only mount one holon per brief anyway.

*The author's, 2026-09-18, dictated; it is the second of [the second thoughts](second-thoughts.md#3-some-levels-are-entered-and-read-not-unfolded) taken up again.*

### 5.1 Two linear holons in one linear are no longer whole

The way to say it: if a holon is made to be linear, and another holon is made to be linear, but they are now put into one linear, then they are no longer whole in terms of their sovereign whole.

*The author's, 2026-09-18, dictated.*

### 5.2 Where the break falls is what is open

We could just decide that files are that breaking point. But that leaves us at a certain limit. It might be exactly the right thing to do.

Or we might find that it is where we decide it is no longer an explicit linear reading experience: that is where you have to open it to read.

*The author's, 2026-09-18, dictated; open.*

### 5.3 Opening the scope costs little, and containment is worth much

Consider how bad it really is to open the scope, because you can always go back. It is not a particularly bad experience.

And it really helps to give containment, because you can be profoundly overwhelmed at this moment. One linear reading experience should not be overwhelming. The rule and its size is forgivable. But now, when you can expand everything at root level, it is just not a good experience.

*The author's, 2026-09-18, dictated.*

There, something like the prose lane with a pane that tells you the depth of where you are. That is explained briefly in the ideas, and it is not a priority right now, but it is certainly to be part of it.

## 7. The canvas gains its second dimension

This idea about the linear, and about actually setting a boundary from one whole linear to another whole linear, also means that the canvas can change. The canvas has felt quite problematic, for it is really just linear except for branching.

So perhaps the canvas can now finally utilise the two dimensions of expression and become more expressive nodes that help us navigate the topology: not everything top to bottom in a straight line, but also left to right. Then it is really clear that sometimes we are structuring holons, and sometimes we are inside a holon looking at its holarchic but linear content.

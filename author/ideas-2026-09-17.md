# Ideas of 2026-09-17

## 1. A sketch or an image as substrate-native presentation

The element of a sketch or an image made a substrate-native presentation, for heroing important content in a freeform way. Say the proof of concept is linked in the rule: an actual canvas to present it would help, not a simple link with a hover and not the gutter, but first-class presentation.

## 2. History is built in

We of course need git support: the data built for the single-page application has to come with holonic, atomic history built in.

Like now, the author wants to highlight the substrate as a diff: the cells go grey, and colour is used instead for comparing the head with a given commit.

*The author's, 2026-09-17, typed after the rename was made one commit; the example is his, the day's refactor being the diff he would look at.*

## 3. Commits are briefs

Commits should be made into briefs themselves, so that the canvas and the prose lane are reused for them.

*The author's, 2026-09-17, typed while the first history view was being built as a widget of its own.*

## 4. Ratification is first class

Ratification, used artfully as a measure in [yesterday's prompts](ideas-2026-09-16.md), needs better integration into the substrate. Today it is either front matter or the italic paragraph at a brief's end; the pilot needs it as a first-class thing.

When the surface later opens for its first write, for it is read-only now, that write is the entry of comments, and also of ratification approvals.

This can be done before AI is given to the client, and it compounds on itself.

# Toolmaking

## 1. Value is made by comprehension, and carried into tools

Humans commonly generate value through comprehension, and toolmaking is what we do with it. A large part of the business world is the making of some tool, which business calls a product. So beside the rule stands a second study of the same kind, an ontological study of how tools are made, and it is the rule turned toward the things we build.

It began inside a project that depends on the rule, the making of a tool for a business, untold here. Nothing in the study is secret, and whatever a dependent project finds that would serve the others migrates into the rule, so from 2026-09-15 toolmaking is built here rather than there.

*The author's, 2026-09-15.*

### 1.1 To comprehend is to seize together

To comprehend is to seize several things together, so comprehension builds on more things by its own definition, or seizes them; which of the two is the rule's own focus is left open.

We began with the tools we made from the environment we were in. One tool changed our language and changed our minds, and from there came more tools.

*The author's, 2026-09-15.*

### 1.2 A tool enables and prevents

A tool can be limiting. It is a construct that enables one thing and prevents another. The chair brings a whole other construct of tools, call it a lifestyle, and it also takes us somewhat away from nature and from our bodies. The Japanese kept a tradition without chairs, a way of living that keeps its connection to our nature.

*The author's, 2026-09-15; the chair is someone's example, recalled and not sourced.*

### 1.3 The rule is a more primal force than a structure

Looking at the nature of knowledge, the rule asks what the most natural way is, and in that it uncovers a respect for our nature. So the rule is not exactly about how we structure knowledge. What is found while understanding how knowledge is structured is more primal: how we respectfully acquire and build new knowledge, and how we respectfully build tools from it.

This belongs in the substrate. The substrate is lossless, it stands at various levels of ratification, and under the rule it compounds by addition and does not suffer by it.

*The author's, 2026-09-15.*

## 2. The primitives

In making tools a few simple ontological primitives are uncovered, and only what is needed at this stage is extracted. No complete body of existing practice is taken on: not the full approach of user experience with its flows, scenarios and personas. The primitives are laid win by win under the rule.

The author's ratified statement of them so far stands beneath, brought whole from the dependent project on 2026-09-15 and absorbed step by step, never all at once.

Goals come first, and the higher goals are unconditional. They may change, but they are much more stable. A goal breaks down into situation and condition, and there it stops being "we want to do this" and becomes "we do this here, because of that".

Work is one word. Studying an existing product finds a great deal of it, under the names user flow, user scenario and user story, and all of it is work. Work is recursive: a piece of work may be reused across other work.

Roles already exist in an existing product. In building one they are not set early on; they come up as the work progresses, step by step.

*Ratified by the author on 2026-09-14 in the level beneath; the words above are the author's of 2026-09-15.*

## 3. Where the substrate of work strains

The dependent project's substrate of work hit this at once, as it began with the highest levels: the work seen from the top, the most important work, which is naturally also a goal. That work is to be broken down, and it is difficult to structure under the rule and the practice, because what stands is the holarchy and links are not that first class.

*The author's, 2026-09-15.*

### 3.1 Which grouping is the win

Pieces of work want grouping, because everything flat is force-fed. But the grouping that looks most win-based may not be. It might be the grouping from one goal, or from another; and in practice roles do different kinds of work because they carry different responsibilities, so the work could be grouped by role, or by goal.

Then, when several pieces of work reuse another, where is that one placed, since the grouping is always a strict hierarchy.

*The author's, 2026-09-15.*

### 3.2 What the medium on the horizon would do, and why not now

Before the rule the author worked on OpenLight, the medium [the rule lays on the horizon](../rule.md#6-how-far-this-goes), and the proof of concept is the deliberate choice not to build it, taking one step at a time with what exists.

In such a substrate, ontological understanding builds archetypes: a typed structure that other holons are instances of. A piece of work, a role, a goal, a fact, a decision could each be one, and then the substrate is viewed from whatever angle is wanted. Doing that in the current substrate would be running ahead, and it is not done.

*The author's, 2026-09-15.*

### 3.3 A child with several parents

What is within reach is this. If a piece of work is placed in the middle of another piece of work, that is what the level beneath is today.

But if the holarchy lets a reader end up at that piece from several places, then the roles, defined at the top of the structure, can lead to it; the goals can lead to it; and a third grouping can lead to the same pieces of work. That does not flatten them and does not type them. It connects several parents to one child.

Whether the other way round is also needed is not known, and perhaps the holarchy's own argument makes it impossible.

The pieces of work might then lie relatively flat in the substrate, in the most important order still, since order remains a problem. But nobody would go to that level, since it force-feeds; a reader takes it from another angle. The heavier substrate stands further down, and at the top the goals are spoken of first and broken down, then the roles, and from either place a reader is welcome to go in and end at the work.

*The author's, 2026-09-15; seen and felt that morning, and still being wrapped around.*

### 3.4 This critiques the substrate

The substrate as it stands is only holarchy and links, and this opens a different door on it. The author needs help to reflect and reason on it, and [the canvas](../poc/surface/canvas.md) is the reader's side of the same question.

*The author's, 2026-09-15.*

### 3.5 Links are not yet said to be wrong

The existing substrate is to be supported to some degree, and the balance is to be found. Reading the rule on GitHub today is a nice experience: the faces are always there, the briefs are always brief, after a few headings the reader goes a level beneath, and pressing that moves them within the file structure. It is not the same as the surface, but it is an improved reading.

So it is not known whether this is a new dynamic in the substrate, or the existing links given more inferred surface, or links reconsidered as a whole.

*The author's, 2026-09-15.*

## 4. What was settled, and what is left open

A child with several parents is [a borrow](../poc/practice.md#37-a-second-lone-link-borrows-and-the-first-place-is-the-home): a lone link last in a brief places a structure there, the first such place is its home, and every later one borrows it. Links in prose stay offers.

A branch is a step whose level holds one brief per condition, each placing or borrowing its path, and the flow continues at the next step of the level above; the level is [one of letters](../poc/practice.md#41-a-level-of-letters-is-a-set), which says only that no condition stands on another, and a flow reads that as a choice. All three are drawn by [the canvas](../poc/surface/canvas.md).

Left open: how the ratified level beneath and the words above become one, brief by brief, in discussion.

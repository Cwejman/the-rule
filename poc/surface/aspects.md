# Aspects

The middle of the page is big type, heading to heading in bold, with less space between, since a reader takes in only so much of a desktop. Around it stand aspects of the same reading: where I am, this level in its order, and a thread being followed. Each can sit small at the edge or be toggled into the middle, where it shows the same headings bigger, with more filled in beside them.

*The author's, 2026-10-04, from [the day's thinking](../../author/ideas-2026-10-04.md#9-big-type-and-the-aspects-come-in-from-the-edge); open, and not built. Where the session proposed, the section says so.*

## 1. Big type, mellowed and activated

Big typography, less spacing and bigger text, because only so much of what is on a desktop is grasped. The text is truly mellowed or activated, and the middle runs heading to heading to heading with bold letters, with a splash of visualisation.

*The author's, 2026-10-04.*

## 2. The aspects come in from the edge

What is looked at has dimensions, holarchies and dimensions, both in browsing and in what is selected. All the briefs of a level in their order, or where I am, can come from the edge into the main view, and the reader toggles which aspect they are looking at.

*The author's, 2026-10-04.*

### 2.1 Where I am

Looked at whole, where I am shows the holarchy at more of its expressiveness: what links to here, and what links to the holarchy I am placed in. It can be held back, or brought into focus with more or fewer details.

*The author's, 2026-10-04.*

### 2.2 This level

What is on this level can be smaller or bigger. Bigger, it may be the same headings, only bigger, with more filled in beside them than before.

*The author's, 2026-10-04.*

### 2.3 A thread

Sometimes the reader is following a thread, and the thread is made available: in a smaller view, or in a fuller one when the details around that fact are wanted.

*The author's, 2026-10-04.*

## 3. Navigation as markers in a filter

Maybe navigation changes, and is really like adding and removing markers in a query filter.

*The author's, 2026-10-04.*

### 3.1 It is OpenLight's read

A read in OpenLight is an intersection of places, `read([project, tasks])`. Each marker would be one place: one added narrows what stands in the middle, one removed widens it. And the sizes of an aspect are the grades [the agent's spec](https://github.com/Cwejman/OpenLight/blob/main/@md/spec/agent.md#the-cycle) already gives, name, summary and body, so that a reading and [a store](../../ideas/store.md) are the same model: places, markers, and how much of each is shown.

*Proposed, the session's, 2026-10-04; not answered.*

## 4. What is open

- Whether the aspects are programs on [the page laid by hand](layout.md), or a reading of their own that the page gives way to.
- What the splash of visualisation draws: the level's order, the links in, or the trail.
- How a marker is shown, added and removed, and whether the way down becomes the markers.

*Open, 2026-10-04.*
